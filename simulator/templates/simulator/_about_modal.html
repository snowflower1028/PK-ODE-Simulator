PK Calculator

Two pharmacokinetic tools, one workspace

The ODE Simulator solves whatever differential system you type: dosing, parameter fitting, sensitivity. The NCA Calculator works the other way, from observed concentrations, and is checked against Phoenix WinNonlin run for run.
