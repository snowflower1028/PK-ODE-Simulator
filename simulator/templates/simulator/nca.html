Choosing the terminal phase

Best fit starts from the last three points and works backwards, keeping whichever stretch has the best adjusted R². Tmax is left out, because a point taken before absorption finishes drags the slope flat and makes the half-life look short.

Judge the result by Span rather than R². Points crowded into a short window line up nicely and give a high R² while barely watching the drug leave.

Below the limit

Where a below-limit sample sits changes what it means, so the three places are set separately.

Between two measurable samples is the one that matters. Calling it zero digs a notch that is not there — and the log-down rule falls back to a straight line when it meets a zero, which deepens the notch further. On a one-compartment bolus with a single such sample, that alone moved AUC by 23%.
